# Portfolio

The personal portfolio site of a frontend lead moving into full-stack AI engineering. It exists to get the owner hired as a Full-Stack AI Engineer (TypeScript + Python).

## Language

### Visitors

**Recruiter**:
A visitor who skims the home page for about 30 seconds and decides whether to pass the owner on.
_Avoid_: HR, reader, user

**Hiring Engineer**:
A visitor who digs into Case Studies, demos and code to judge technical depth.
_Avoid_: tech reviewer, developer, reader

### Work

**Project**:
A piece of the owner's own work featured on the site.
_Avoid_: app, product, repo

**Live Project**:
A Project with a working public demo.

**In-Progress Project**:
A Project shown honestly as unfinished: it has no live demo yet and links to its design notes instead.
_Avoid_: coming soon, WIP

**Project Card**:
The short summary of one Project on the home page.
_Avoid_: tile, teaser

**Case Study**:
The detail page for one Project: problem, what the AI may and may not do, how it was measured, trade-offs and limits.
_Avoid_: project page, write-up, deep dive

### Theme

**Hand-off**:
The moment an AI passes a decision to a person because it is unsure or the decision is risky. The theme that ties all Projects together.
_Avoid_: escalation, fallback

**Console Panel**:
The dark, glowing section that frames AI work, set against the warm editorial page.
_Avoid_: dark card, terminal
